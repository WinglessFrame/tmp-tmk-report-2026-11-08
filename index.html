How to read the scores

TMK L1 and L2 lower is better

Meta's video fingerprint, comparing the whole clip. L1 is the overall look, L2 is how the look changes over time. Both run from 0 to 1, where 1 is identical. Meta treats two videos as the same when both are 0.7 or higher.

PDQ higher is better

Meta's image fingerprint, checked on two frames per second. It counts how many of 256 bits differ from the source. Under 31 a frame counts as the same picture; about 128 is what two unrelated pictures score. We show the median over the clip.

Win

Reelforge is further from the source than MetaGhost on all three numbers: lower L1, lower L2 and higher PDQ. In the tables, reelforge's number is green where it beats MetaGhost's.

What could not be made identical in MetaGhost

  • Zoom aim and crop position. MetaGhost picks a random point each render and has no option to set it. Reelforge picks it from the clip's content. The amount of zoom and crop is the same.
  • Camera-sway path. The amount (% of frame) is the same, but MetaGhost's sway speed and starting phase are internal and random.
  • Video bitrate. MetaGhost treats it as a cap; its hardware encoder actually wrote about 7.5 Mbps. Reelforge encodes at the set value.
  • Pitch. Reelforge only; MetaGhost has no pitch option (0 in both).
  • Units. Aspect is a % in MetaGhost and a factor in reelforge (MG 1.2% = 1.012); the table shows both where they differ.
  • Rotation, perspective, lens correction and warp stay on in MetaGhost (at A: −0.15°, 2.5, 0.0025, 0.6). Reelforge's Spoof no longer applies them because they bent the text, so MetaGhost has four extra distortions here.
  • Speed is pinned to MetaGhost's values in both tools. Reelforge's own Spoof range is now 1.02–1.04, which this section doesn't use.

2 · Real use: random values

Twenty full-frame sources in two batches of ten, one render each, the way the tools are used day to day. Reelforge runs its built-in Spoof (Metaghost) profile, drawing its own random values. MetaGhost runs simple spoof mode as selected in its UI, also random. Simple mode applies rotation, perspective, lens correction, a per-edge random crop, an aspect crop, a pixel-shift ripple, colour and noise tweaks and a speed change, but no zoom, camera motion or grain. Reelforge wins 20 of 20. Median over all 20: MetaGhost 0.954 / 0.954 / 60, reelforge 0.589 / 0.591 / 114.
